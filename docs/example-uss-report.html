Japanese Equity Research · 4732 · Used-vehicle auctions

USS Co., Ltd.

株式会社ユー・エス・エス

Business

USS operates used-vehicle auctions that match dealers and other members through physical sites and remote bidding. It earns consignment fees, contract-completion fees and successful-bid fees. Group businesses also purchase and sell used vehicles and recycle vehicles and industrial equipment.

Issuer sources · uss-auctions, uss-business

Research notes

Illustrative research prompts

Investment thesis

A dense auction network could support a durable fee business if listing volumes and completion rates hold as fees rise.

Supporting evidence

Compare vehicle consignments, successful contracts, completion rates and fee revenue with the issuer’s operating disclosures. Check whether annual cash flow supports reported profit.

Catalysts

Track auction volumes and the impact of fee changes on both sides of the marketplace.

Risks

A tighter used-vehicle supply or dealer migration could reduce throughput. Recycling and vehicle resale add exposure to prices and mix.

Evidence that would invalidate the thesis

Sustained declines in consignments and completion rates following fee increases, coupled with weaker operating margins, would weaken the network-pricing thesis.

Methodology

  • Revenue growth divides the current comparable full-year revenue by the prior full-year revenue and subtracts one. The two margins divide operating profit and attributable profit by revenue, respectively.
  • Cash capex is cash paid for property, plant and equipment plus intangible assets. Cash flow after capital expenditure is operating cash flow minus those purchases.
  • Interest-bearing debt sums separately disclosed borrowings, bonds and recognized lease liabilities where applicable. Net debt subtracts closing cash and cash equivalents. The scope excludes unrecognized lease commitments and unclassified other liabilities.
  • EPS reconciliation divides attributable profit by weighted-average ordinary shares. Allowed rounding is JPY 0.005 plus JPY 1 million divided by the weighted-average shares.
  • Forecast revenue = base revenue × (1 + revenue growth).
  • Forecast attributable profit = forecast revenue × attributable net margin.
  • Forecast basic EPS = forecast attributable profit ÷ projected weighted-average ordinary shares.
  • Implied price: forecast EPS × assumed forward P/E. P/E valuation is unavailable when forecast EPS is zero or negative.
  • Price upside/downside = implied price ÷ manual reference share price − 1. The price comparison excludes dividends and does not establish a holding period.
  • Sensitivity varies attributable net margin by up to ±2 percentage points and the multiple from 60% to 140% of the selected scenario, holding forecast growth and shares fixed.

Coverage and limitations

  • The oldest fiscal year has no growth comparison in the bundled data.
  • MonotaRO's Japanese December 2022 release reports that adopting the revenue recognition standard had no effect on profit or loss. The February 3, 2023 correction affects financing cash flow line labels and standalone income, outside the selected metrics.
  • A balance-sheet line missing from a release is left blank in the component data. A dash on a reported line is preserved in provenance and treated as nil. Debt totals use the reviewed set of separately identified balance-sheet lines.
  • The issuer translations report financial amounts rounded down to millions of yen. The historical view uses current-year releases and identifies split normalization explicitly.
  • This offline dataset contains annual statements. Reference prices are manual inputs. Scenario prices and rationales are research assumptions.
